Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What Local Post Hub is

Local Post Hub is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It is used by one person to publish their own content to their own accounts.

YouTube API Services

Local Post Hub uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access, use and store

  • Accessed: after you sign in with Google and approve access, the tool receives an OAuth access token and refresh token, and reads your YouTube channel's ID and title (channels.list).
  • Used: to upload videos you choose to your own channel with the title, description, tags and privacy setting you choose (videos.insert), and optionally to set a thumbnail (thumbnails.set).
  • Stored: the tokens, channel ID and title are stored only in a local file on your own computer. Nothing is stored on any server operated by Local Post Hub, and no YouTube data is stored beyond that.
  • Shared: no data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube, as part of the API requests above.

Cookies and tracking

Local Post Hub does not use cookies, analytics or any tracking, and does not place or read information on your device other than its own local data file.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • You can revoke Local Post Hub's access to your Google account at any time via the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Choosing "Disconnect" in the tool immediately deletes the stored tokens and channel data from your computer. Revoking access through Google also stops all access; any remaining local copy can be deleted by deleting the tool's data folder.
