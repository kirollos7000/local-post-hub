Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Local Post Hub runs entirely on the owner's own computer. Access tokens and basic profile information (account id and display name) received from connected platforms are stored only locally on that computer and are used solely to publish content the owner chooses.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent anywhere other than the official API of the platform being posted to. No analytics or tracking is used.

Access can be revoked at any time in the connected platform's settings; the locally stored tokens can be deleted at any time.
